## About

This is the landing page for Vector. Its job is to introduce the tool to developers and explain,
in plain terms, why it exists and what it does.

The page walks a visitor through the whole story of Vector:

- **The problem** — why a normal Azure DevOps → GitHub migration quietly costs you your green squares, leaks access tokens, and stalls on large repositories.
- **How it works** — the three-stage migration, explained simply: mirror the history in, rewrite your identity, then push it safely to GitHub.
- **Quick start** — how to run the tool, with the commands for macOS, Linux, and Windows.
- **Why it's safe** — the guarantees that make Vector trustworthy: zero-token, non-destructive, deterministic, and incremental.

The design is dark and developer-focused, with a GitHub-style contribution-green accent that ties
back to the "keep your green squares" idea at the heart of the tool.
